⚽ Soccer World Cup

Reading Groups

Red Group

I Like Soccer

See the big green grass. This is a soccer field. Look at the ball. It is round and white.

The happy children run fast. They like to play a game. They kick the ball with their feet. Kick, kick, kick!

The boy runs. The girl runs. They push the ball into the big net. It is a goal! Yay! Everyone is happy. Soccer is a fun game to play with friends.

Comprehension Questions

1. Literal: What colour is the grass?

2. Literal: What do the children kick?

3. Literal: Where does the ball go to make a goal?

4. Vocabulary: What does fast mean?

5. Inference: How do the kids feel when they play?

6. Inference: Why do the children run around?

Yellow Group

The Big Soccer Game

Look at the big soccer field. It is very big. Children are running and kicking a ball. They are playing soccer. Soccer is a fun sport.

Many people play soccer. Soccer players wear red shirts. Some wear white shirts. The players run very fast. They kick the ball hard. They try to put the ball in the goal. The ball goes in! The people say "Goal!"

The World Cup is a big soccer game. The best soccer teams play together. Countries from all over the world play. The people watch and cheer. They are very happy. The winner gets a big trophy. It is golden and shiny. Everyone wants to win the World Cup!

Soccer is the best sport. Do you like soccer? Many children love to play soccer with their friends. Playing soccer is fun and good for you.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What is a goal in soccer?

2. Literal: What colour shirts do the text say soccer players wear?

3. Vocabulary: What is a trophy?

4. Literal: What is the World Cup?

5. Inference: Why do people cheer at the World Cup?

6. Inference: Why do soccer players run fast?

Green Group

The World Cup Adventure

Maria was excited. She was going to watch the World Cup! The World Cup is the biggest soccer tournament in the world. Countries from every area send their best players. The game would be at a big stadium with thousands of people.

Maria wore her country's colours. Her shirt was green and yellow. She painted her face with stripes. Her family was so excited too. They carried a big flag and wore matching jerseys. Everyone wanted to cheer for their team. The field was perfectly green and the scoreboard was huge.

Players ran onto the field in their coloured uniforms. The crowd started to shout loudly. Maria's heart beat fast with excitement. Her team kicked the ball and scored a goal! The final whistle blew and Maria's team won! They were champions! Everyone danced and celebrated. Watching the World Cup was the best day ever.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What is a tournament?

2. Literal: What did Maria wear to the stadium?

3. Vocabulary: What are champions?

4. Literal: What did Maria's team do at the end of the game?

5. Inference: How did Maria feel when her team scored a goal?

6. Inference: Why did Maria's family dress in matching jerseys and carry a flag?

Blue Group

The World Cup and Soccer Stars

The World Cup is the biggest soccer game in the world. It happens every four years. The best soccer teams from many countries play against each other. Each team wants to win the trophy. The trophy is very special and shiny.

Kids around the world love to watch the World Cup. They wear their country's colours and cheer loudly for their team. The stadiums are full of happy people singing and shouting. Families watch together on TV. Everyone feels proud when their team scores a goal.

Playing in the World Cup is a big dream for soccer players. Some players become famous because they play so well. Other players become heroes to kids everywhere. They show that hard work and practice help you get better at soccer. Many children play soccer because they want to be in the World Cup one day.

The World Cup brings people together. People from different places become friends when they watch soccer. Soccer makes everyone happy. The World Cup shows why soccer is the most popular sport in the world.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What does famous mean?

2. Literal: How often does the World Cup happen?

3. Vocabulary: What is a hero?

4. Literal: Why do children watch the World Cup?

5. Inference: Why do you think children want to play soccer in the World Cup?

6. Inference: How does the World Cup bring people together?

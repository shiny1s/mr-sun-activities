7. ⭐ Bonus – What Type of Sentence?

There are four types of sentences. Choose the right one for each.

  • Declarative – a statement (tells us something)
  • Interrogative – a question (asks something)
  • Exclamatory – an exclamation (strong feeling)
  • Imperative – a command (tells you to do something)

Please sit down.

The weather is sunny.

Are you coming to the party?

What a beautiful sunset!

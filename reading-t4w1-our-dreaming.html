The Spirit of Country

For many Aboriginal peoples, Country is very special. It is not just the dirt and rocks. Country can be connected to the spirit of ancestors. It can also include language, art and dance.

When people walk on Country, they may feel connected to living things. They listen to the land, and the land helps them learn.

Green Group

A Deep Bond

Connection to Country is a deep bond for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. Country is much more than a place on a map. It holds history, traditions and spirit.

It includes animals, plants, skies and waterways. Families pass down knowledge about how to care for the land. This connection helps keep culture alive and strong.

Blue Group

A Living Connection

For First Nations peoples, the concept of Country is complex and deeply meaningful. It extends beyond the physical environment of mountains, rivers and trees. Country can encompass spirituality, language, law and family ties.

Many people describe Country as a living connection that supports identity and wellbeing. Ancestors have walked these lands for many thousands of years, leaving a rich cultural heritage. Maintaining a profound connection to Country is important for wellbeing, identity and passing wisdom to future generations.

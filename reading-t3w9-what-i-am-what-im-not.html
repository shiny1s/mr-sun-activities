📖 The Library Kid

Reading Groups

Red Group

The Library Kid

I am not a sport kid. I do not run on a court.

I am not a tough kid. I do not pick fights.

I am a library kid. I love books. I read a lot.

Comprehension Questions

Vocabulary: What is a court?

Literal: What does the kid NOT do?

Vocabulary: What does tough mean?

Literal: What kind of kid is the speaker?

Inference: Where does the kid like to be?

Inference: What does the kid like doing?

Yellow Group

The Library Kid

I am not a sporty kid who loves to chase a ball. I do not run around a track all day. I am not a rough kid who picks fights or breaks the rules.

I am a library kid. I am a daydreamer who loves stories. I can read three books in a single week. What kind of kid are you?

Comprehension Questions

Vocabulary: What is a track?

Literal: How many books does the kid read in a week?

Vocabulary: What is a daydreamer?

Literal: What does the sporty kid chase?

Inference: How does the kid feel about breaking rules?

Inference: Why is the speaker a "library kid"?

Green Group

The Library Kid

Some kids love to race around a running track or chase a ball on a basketball court. That is not me. Other kids try to look tough and mean, breaking the rules just to cause trouble. I am definitely not a rebel kid.

Instead, I am a proud library kid. I am a daydreamer and a weaver of words. I can easily devour three books in a single week. I find true magic in stories.

Comprehension Questions

Vocabulary: What does rebel mean?

Literal: What do other kids try to look like?

Vocabulary: What does devour mean in this text?

Literal: Where does the speaker find magic?

Inference: Why does the speaker call themselves a "weaver of words"?

Inference: How does the speaker feel about their identity?

Blue Group

The Library Kid

Not everyone fits into the same mould. While many children thrive on the thrill of a chase or the competition of a sports court, I have never been athletically inclined. Furthermore, I have no desire to be the tough rebel who breaks the rules or seeks out trouble on the playground.

Instead, I proudly identify as a library kid. I am a daydreamer, a quiet geek, and a weaver of words. For me, the greatest adventure lies not in a physical race, but within the pages of a well written story. I can comfortably devour multiple novels in a week. We all have our unique passions, so what is yours?

Comprehension Questions

Vocabulary: What does inclined mean?

Literal: Where does the greatest adventure lie for the speaker?

Vocabulary: What does thrive mean?

Literal: What does the speaker not want to seek out on the playground?

Inference: What is the main idea of the first sentence?

Inference: How does the speaker view reading compared to sports?

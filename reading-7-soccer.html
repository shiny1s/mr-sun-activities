Yellow Group

Soccer Training with Sam

Sam was a young soccer player. She trained on a large, grassy field with her team every day. The sun was bright and hot, but Sam was not tired.

Her coach had great energy and was kind. She listened to his instructions with focus. The steady voice told Sam that she was getting better every day. The kick - pass - score routine showed Sam that practice makes perfect.

Word Power & Comprehension

1. Word Hunt: Find the word focus in the text. If Sam has focus, does it mean she is daydreaming, or she is paying full attention?

Green Group

The Game of Soccer

Soccer is a popular sport played around the world, and it is highly challenging to master. There are different types of soccer players, which are called positions. Sam is a forward. Forwards mostly focus on scoring goals and attacking the opposing team.

They need strong legs that are fast and powerful, and they use their bodies to shield the ball from defenders. Soccer players are important team members. They work together to win games and support their teammates, but individual effort and skill are also valued because talented players can create winning moments.

Soccer Detective Challenges

1. Vocabulary Check: Look at the phrase team member. Based on the text, what does a team member do?

2. Fact Retrieval: What is a "position" in soccer? Which specific position does Sam play?

3. Deep Thinking: The text says forwards need strong legs and speed. Using clues from the passage, why do you think these skills are important for winning soccer games?

Blue Group

Authorship and the Guardian of the Ecosystem

When Favel Parrett wrote the book Wandi, she wanted to change our perspective on wild dingoes. For over 200 years, dingoes have been widely misunderstood because they look like domestic dogs, yet they are actually crucial to the Australian ecosystem.

As an apex predator, the dingo acts as an environmental guardian by keeping invasive pests in check and preventing kangaroo overpopulation. Favel Parrett has immense authority on this subject matter. Before writing, she worked directly as a volunteer at the Dingo Discovery Sanctuary. Because she has real-world experience observing alpine dingo eco-types, readers trust her authorship and find her arguments highly credible. We must listen to her message so we can preserve these unique creatures for future generations.

Authorship and the Passion for the Game

When Alex Rivera wrote the book Sam's Soccer Journey, she wanted to change how young people think about team sports. For many years, soccer has been widely enjoyed because it brings people together, yet many players don't understand the psychology behind what makes great teams work.

As a forward and team player, Sam must balance individual skill with team collaboration. Alex Rivera has immense authority on this subject matter. Before writing, she worked as a professional soccer player and coach. Because she has real-world experience playing competitive soccer, readers trust her authorship and find her advice highly credible. We must listen to her message so young players can develop both technique and teamwork for future success.

Author's Craft & Analysis

1. Vocabulary Mastery: Define authority and authorship in your own words based on how they are used in the text.

2. Language Feature Hunt: Locate and copy down one declarative sentence from the text that states a clear fact about soccer.

3. Evaluative Critique: What was Alex Rivera's primary purpose in writing Sam's Soccer Journey? Do you think sharing a story from a single player's perspective is an effective way to teach young people about soccer? Explain your viewpoint.

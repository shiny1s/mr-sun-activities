Persuasive Writing - Anecdotes and Examples

Persuasive Writing Unit  |  Adding Evidence to Reasons

1. Concept: Examples

When we write a persuasive speech, we must prove our reasons. One way is to use an Example. An example gives a specific fact or detail to show why you are right. We often start with "For example..." or "For instance..."

Mini Activity: Choose the correct words to complete the example.

1) Reason: We should eat fresh fruit.
, oranges are packed with healthy vitamins.
2) Reason: Reading books is fun.
, you can read about space or deep oceans.

2. Concept: Anecdotes

Another way to prove your reason is to use an Anecdote. An anecdote is a short personal story about something that happened to you. It helps the reader feel what you felt. We often start with "I remember when..." or "Once, I..."

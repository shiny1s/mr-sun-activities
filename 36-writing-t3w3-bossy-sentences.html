Persuasive Writing – Bossy Sentences (Imperatives)

Persuasive Writing Unit  |  Imperatives & Calls to Action

In persuasive writing, we use bossy sentences (imperative sentences) to tell the reader exactly what to do. They always start with a strong action verb and give a clear call to action, like "Stop polluting our creeks!"

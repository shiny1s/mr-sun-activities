The Future of Creation - Reading Groups

Red Group

3D Printers Are Magic

3D printers are amazing machines. They are not like normal printers. Normal printers only print flat pictures on paper. A 3D printer can print real toys, tools, and shapes!

You absolutely must try using one. It feels like magic. If you can imagine a toy, a 3D printer can make it real. You should learn about them today because they are the best tools ever made!

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What is a machine?

2. Literal: What do normal printers print on?

3. Vocabulary: What does imagine mean?

4. Literal: What can a 3D printer make?

5. Inference: Why does the author think 3D printers are like magic?

6. Inference: Why does the author want you to learn about them?

Yellow Group

Save the World with 3D Printing

You need to know about 3D printing because it is changing the world! Instead of buying everything from a store, you can just print what you need right at home. This is called manufacturing. 3D printers use melted plastic to build things layer by layer.

It is so much better for our planet! When we print at home, we save gas because delivery trucks do not have to drive to our houses. Everyone should have a 3D printer to help save the Earth and invent incredible new things.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What does manufacturing mean?

2. Literal: How does a 3D printer build things?

3. Vocabulary: What does incredible mean?

4. Literal: What do we save when delivery trucks do not drive to our houses?

5. Inference: Why is printing at home better for the planet?

6. Inference: What is the main reason the author wants you to have a 3D printer?

Green Group

The Jobs of Tomorrow

If you want a brilliant future, you absolutely must learn how to use a 3D printer! These machines are not just for printing fun toys or simple models. Today, brilliant doctors are using 3D printers to make perfectly fitted artificial arms and legs for people who need them. Engineers use them to build intricate parts for spaceships that explore our solar system!

By learning this technology now, you are preparing yourself for the most important jobs of the future. We need thinkers and builders who can solve big problems. Do not get left behind in this exciting new era. Start designing your own 3D models today and become a creator, not just a consumer. If you can dream it, you can print it. You could be the one to invent the next big thing!

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What does technology mean?

2. Literal: What are doctors making with 3D printers?

3. Vocabulary: What is a consumer?

4. Literal: What do engineers build with 3D printers?

5. Inference: What does the author mean by "Do not get left behind"?

6. Inference: Why is it better to be a "creator" than a "consumer"?

Blue Group

The New Industrial Revolution

We are quickly entering a new industrial revolution, and 3D printing is leading the way. You really must learn about this incredible tool if you want to be ready for the future! Traditional factories that make things are often very slow, create a lot of waste, and can be harmful to the environment. 3D printing, on the other hand, allows for total customisation. You can carefully design a product that perfectly fits your exact needs and print it right away.

This amazing technology has the power to change how we move things around the world. Right now, massive cargo ships travel across our oceans, creating a lot of pollution. Instead of shipping finished goods all the way across the globe, we can simply send digital files over the internet and print them locally. By using 3D printing, you are taking the power away from giant companies and putting it firmly back into your own hands.

Furthermore, the materials used in modern 3D printing are becoming much better for the earth. Smart scientists are developing special plastics made from corn starch and recycled materials. This means you can create complex, strong objects without adding to the giant rubbish dumps that hurt our planet. It is our shared responsibility to support these eco-friendly ideas and protect our environment.

You owe it to yourself and future generations to master 3D design and become a true creator. We no longer have to buy the exact same boring items that everyone else has. By using the power of a 3D printer, you have the amazing ability to shape the physical world around you. Do not just sit back and wait for the future to happen; take charge, get creative, and actively print it!

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What does customisation mean?

2. Literal: What does the author say traditional factories are?

3. Vocabulary: What is a revolution?

4. Literal: What can we do instead of shipping goods on cargo ships?

5. Inference: How are the materials in 3D printing becoming better for the earth?

6. Inference: What is the author's primary goal in this passage?

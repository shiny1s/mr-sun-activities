🎮 Roblox

Reading Groups

Red Group

Roblox is Fun

Roblox is a game platform. People play games on it. You can make a character. Your character is called an avatar. You can dress your avatar in cool clothes.

You can play with friends. You can run and jump in games. You can build things. You can pretend and have fun. Roblox has many different games to play. There is always something new and exciting to do!

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What is a platform?

2. Literal: What is an avatar?

3. Vocabulary: What does exciting mean?

4. Literal: Can you play with friends in Roblox?

5. Inference: Why do you think people like playing Roblox?

6. Inference: Why would you want to dress your avatar in cool clothes?

Yellow Group

Playing in Roblox

Roblox is an online game platform where players can explore different worlds. Each game in Roblox is unique. Some games are about racing, while others are about building or solving puzzles. Players create an avatar to represent them in the games.

You can make your avatar look different by adding clothes, accessories, and hairstyles. When you play with friends, you can work together to complete challenges. Many players enjoy Roblox because there is always a new game to try. You can also watch what other players have created and learn from their ideas.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What does unique mean?

2. Literal: What types of games are mentioned in Roblox?

3. Vocabulary: What are accessories?

4. Literal: What can you do with your avatar in Roblox?

5. Inference: Why is it helpful to watch what other players have created?

6. Inference: Why do you think working together with friends is fun in Roblox?

Green Group

Games and Friends in Roblox

Roblox has many types of different games that players can choose from. Some games are about solving puzzles or completing missions. Other games let you build things, race cars, or act out roles in different worlds. Each game has its own rules and challenges to complete.

When you play games in Roblox, you often play with other people. You can work as a team to reach goals and solve problems together. Working with friends helps you finish difficult challenges. Many Roblox games teach you new skills as you play. Some games teach you about building, while others help you learn to plan and work with others. The more you play, the better you get at different skills.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What is a mission?

2. Literal: What are some types of games you can play in Roblox?

3. Vocabulary: What does challenge mean?

4. Literal: What happens when you work as a team in Roblox?

5. Inference: How do Roblox games teach you new skills?

6. Inference: Why do you think getting better at skills takes practice?

Blue Group

Exploring the World of Roblox

Roblox is a platform where players explore thousands of different games and experiences. Each game is designed by creators who use special tools to build virtual worlds. Players can customize their avatars with clothing, accessories, and animations to make them unique.

Roblox offers games in many categories. Some games focus on adventure and exploration, while others feature strategy, sports, or simulation. Players can team up with friends to complete tasks and overcome challenges. The social aspect is important because teamwork helps players succeed. Many players enjoy creating their own games on the platform, which makes Roblox both a playground and a creative tool.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What does customize mean?

2. Literal: What types of game categories does Roblox have?

3. Vocabulary: What is teamwork?

4. Literal: Who creates the games on Roblox?

5. Inference: Why is the social aspect of Roblox important?

6. Inference: How is Roblox both a playground and a creative tool?

Reading Groups

Red Group

Michael Jackson

Michael Jackson was a singer. He sang and danced. People loved to watch him.

He wore a white glove. He had a big hat. He made music that was fun to hear.

He could do a special dance move. It was called the moonwalk. People thought it was amazing.

Michael Jackson was called the King of Pop. He is still famous today.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What is a singer?

2. Literal: What did Michael Jackson wear on his hand?

3. Literal: What was Michael Jackson's special dance move called?

4. Literal: What was Michael Jackson called?

5. Inference: How did people feel when they watched Michael Jackson dance?

6. Inference: Why do people still know who Michael Jackson is today?

Green Group

Michael Jackson's Life and Music

Michael Jackson was born in 1958. He started to perform when he was very young. His whole family sang and danced together. They were called the Jackson 5. Young Michael was amazing on stage.

When he grew up, Michael Jackson became a solo artist. He made his own albums with wonderful songs. His album called "Thriller" was the best-selling album of all time. Millions of people bought his music and loved to listen to it.

Michael Jackson was famous for his special dance moves. He invented the moonwalk. This was a move where he appeared to glide backward smoothly. When people saw this, they were amazed! He also wore a sparkly jacket and a white glove when he performed. His style was unique and special.

Michael Jackson's songs made people happy and want to dance. Children and adults all over the world loved his music. He gave concerts that people travelled far to watch. Michael Jackson was one of the greatest entertainers of all time.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What is a solo artist?

2. Literal: What was the Jackson 5?

3. Vocabulary: What is the moonwalk?

4. Literal: What was the name of Michael Jackson's best-selling album?

5. Inference: How did people feel when they saw Michael Jackson's moonwalk?

6. Inference: Why did Michael Jackson become famous around the world?

Blue Group

The King of Pop: Michael Jackson's Impact

Michael Jackson was born on August 29, 1958, in Indiana. He was part of a musical family called the Jackson 5. As a young boy, he performed with his brothers. His talent was noticed very early. He became a star while still a child.

As an adult, Michael Jackson became a legendary performer. He sold over 350 million records worldwide. His album "Thriller" changed the music industry. It combined pop, rock, and rhythm and blues. The album won many awards and broke sales records.

Michael Jackson's dancing was revolutionary. He invented the moonwalk, a move that made him appear to float backward. His music videos were like short movies with amazing choreography. He wore a sequined jacket and a sparkly glove that became his trademark. People everywhere copied his style and his moves.

Michael Jackson's influence went beyond music. He inspired artists everywhere to be creative and original. He used his platform to speak about peace and helping others. Michael Jackson is remembered as one of the greatest entertainers in history.

Comprehension Questions

1. Vocabulary: What does legendary mean?

2. Literal: How many records did Michael Jackson sell worldwide?

3. Vocabulary: What is a trademark?

4. Literal: What made the "Thriller" album special and different?

5. Inference: Why did people copy Michael Jackson's dance moves and style?

6. Inference: What does the text suggest about Michael Jackson's importance?
